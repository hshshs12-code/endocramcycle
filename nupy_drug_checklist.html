Drug Checklist

Every drug you need to know for NUPY Mini 1, grouped by disease and by class. Tick each one off once you know it; the small tags show which lecture teaches it. The lists come from the official drug-list slides (9.09 Neuropharmacology, 10.09 Local Anesthetics, the opioid classification table) plus every drug taught in the Movement Disorders and Muscle Weakness pharmacology workshops, the headache, sleep, eye, autonomic and other lectures, and the HY DOC (pages 1–100), and every card in the NUPY Mini 1 Drugs Quizlet set (tagged “Quizlet” where a drug is only on the Quizlet). For mechanisms and details, see the Drugs Table.

A drug that fits two groups is listed where it is mainly taught, with its other uses in the grey note. Combined lines (e.g. "Oxycodone · hydromorphone · oxymorphone") are listed together because the lectures teach them as one group.
