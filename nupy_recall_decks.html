The Anki decks, three ways: choose, match or type.

Every card of the compact cloze decks as one question — same text, same blanks, same slides. Multiple choice: pick the option that fills the numbered blanks. Drag & match: drag each item from the bank into the right blank (tap an item then tap a blank on a phone), then check. Type it: write your own answer for every blank; it is auto-graded by similarity to the card's wording, and you can overrule the grade. Each mode keeps its own progress and flags in this browser.
