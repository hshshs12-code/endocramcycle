Details preserved from the document
  • Smell: no brainstem nucleus and no thalamic relay before primary olfactory cortex. The figure also labels the olfactory tubercle, amygdala, entorhinal cortex and contralateral anterior olfactory nucleus; the notes link smell to memory.

Clinical links preserved
  • Thalamic pain syndrome: posterolateral thalamic injury, often posterior thalamoperforating occlusion → initial numbness/tingling, later contralateral burning/aching/stabbing pain, allodynia and hyperalgesia; temperature abnormalities may accompany it.

Cochlear and reflex details
  • Mechanical pressure return: scala vestibuli and scala tympani communicate at helicotrema; pressure returns through scala tympani to round window.
  • Frequency map: high-frequency / high-pitch sounds act at the cochlear base; low-frequency / low-pitch sounds travel toward the apex.

  • Acoustic reflex: source associates stapedius contraction with suppression of loud low-frequency sounds (<1000 Hz), protection and improved speech discrimination in background noise. It does not supply a complete afferent-to-efferent reflex arc.
