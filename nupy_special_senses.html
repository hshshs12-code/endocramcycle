eyeVisual System & Reflexes — reflex arcs, abnormal pupils, gaze control [eye · physiology / neuro-ophthalmology]· 11REFLEX SEQUENCES as ordered cards (afferent → centre → efferent), then a lesion table.

Pupillary light reflex

  1. AfferentLight → retinal ganglion cells → optic nerve → chiasm (partial crossing) → optic tract.
  2. CentreFibres bypass the LGN → pretectal nuclei (midbrain) → BILATERAL Edinger-Westphal nuclei.
  3. EfferentPreganglionic parasympathetics in CN III → ciliary ganglion → short ciliary nerves → sphincter pupillae.
  4. ResultDirect + consensual constriction.
  5. Lesion: optic nerveLight in the bad eye → neither pupil constricts; light in the good eye → both do.
  6. Lesion: oculomotor nerveThat pupil never constricts (direct or consensual); the other always does.

auditoryAuditory System — ear anatomy, cochlea, pathway [auditory · anatomy / histology / physiology]· 9STRUCTURE TABLE, then the pathway as a sequence card.

Central auditory pathway

  1. 1Hair cells → spiral ganglion → cochlear nerve (enters at the cerebellopontine angle).
  2. 2Ventral (bilateral projection) and dorsal (contralateral) cochlear nuclei — the only nuclei WITHOUT binaural input.
  3. 3Trapezoid body (crossing ventral fibres) → superior olive: medial = interaural TIME difference, lateral = interaural LEVEL difference.
  4. 4Lateral lemniscus → inferior colliculus → brachium → medial geniculate body.
  5. 5Auditory radiations → transverse gyrus of Heschl (41, 42).
  6. RuleUnilateral deafness only with a lesion of the nerve or cochlear nuclei; above that every station is binaural.
