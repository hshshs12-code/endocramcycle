  6. Decidua basalis (maternal) plus chorion frondosum (fetal) form the definitive placenta

NEW Sequence recap:
  1. GnRH pulses from the hypothalamus drive anterior pituitary FSH and LH
  2. FSH recruits follicles; granulosa cells aromatize theca-derived androgen to estradiol
  3. Rising estradiol initially suppresses FSH (negative feedback), selecting a dominant follicle
  4. Estradiol above threshold for ~48 h flips to positive feedback, triggering the LH surge
  5. LH surge causes ovulation ~36 h later; the follicle becomes the corpus luteum
  6. Corpus luteum secretes progesterone, converting endometrium to secretory phase
  7. Without hCG the corpus luteum regresses, progesterone falls, and menses follows
