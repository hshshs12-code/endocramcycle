Psych Diagnosis Map

Every psychiatric condition taught in Mini 2, sorted the way you'd reason through it on an exam. Diagnose it walks you through one question at a time. The Flowchart shows every branch at once. Families groups the conditions into colour-coded clusters. Time thresholds lines up every duration cut-off on one ruler. Schizo- separates everything with "schizo" in the name. Treatment groups conditions by what they're treated with. Look-alikes groups the ones that get confused, with the one tell for each, plus a cover-and-check mode for cramming. Tap any condition for its full criteria from the Pathology Table.

The whole diagnostic flowchart

Dark boxes are the questions you ask; white tags are the answers; coloured pills are the diagnoses (colour = family). Click a question to fold or unfold its branch, or a pill for full criteria.

    Families at a glance

    Each card is one DSM family: the idea that defines the family, then its members with their hallmark and cut-off. Members are in the order you'd separate them.

    Time thresholds on one ruler

    Many psych diagnoses differ mainly in how long symptoms last. Bars start at the minimum duration; a fading end means "or longer". The scale is logarithmic so days and years both fit. Faded bars are the shorter child/adolescent cut-offs.

    Look-alikes: what separates them

    Each card groups conditions that get confused, names the one axis that separates them, then gives each one its tell. For cramming, switch on Cover the tells, say the difference out loud, tap to check, then tick Got it.

    Treatment at a glance

    By treatment lists each drug class or therapy with the conditions it treats. By condition flips it round. Start with the red Never do card. To cram, turn on Cover, recall the answer, then tap to check.

    Criteria, durations and differentiators come from the Mini 2 Pathology Table (lecture content); the flowchart's question order follows the usual DSM-5-TR reasoning (rule out substance/medical first, then mood vs psychosis, then duration).
