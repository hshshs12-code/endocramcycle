Tiers were set by how each drug is taught: own section with mechanism in a pharmacology session, official drug-list slide, review/practice question or the Arbat drug summary = need to know; a single drug→disease line in a clinical lecture = know what it's for; named once as an alternative, in handwriting only, or “addressed elsewhere” = skip. A drug that fits two groups is listed where it is mainly taught, with its other uses in the grey note. Combined lines (e.g. "Oxycodone · hydromorphone · oxymorphone") are listed together because the lectures teach them as one group.
