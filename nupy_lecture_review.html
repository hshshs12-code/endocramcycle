Lecture Review Checklist

What to review when you come back to a lecture. The main checklist (100 items) is built only from Neuroscience.pdf; the lecture slides were used to title and organise it. Slide-only ideas sit in a separate collapsed list under each lecture (50 in total) and never count toward progress. Opening an answer does not change an item’s status — you set it.

Progress, notes and dates are saved in this browser on this device only — use Export to back up or move them.
Sources: Neuroscience.pdf (56 PDF pages; p. 55 is blank) is the primary source for every main item and answer. Page numbers are PDF pages. Lecture references are lecture-PDF page numbers, not printed slide numbers. Mapping between the two was done by text matching and, for image-only pages, by topic — stated per lecture. Where the notes and slides disagree, the item says so instead of choosing.
